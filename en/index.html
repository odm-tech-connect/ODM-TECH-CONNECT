About the initiative

A local connection for international business.

ODM TECH CONNECT aims to help international companies explore business opportunities, develop professional relationships and build connections in Guinea and the wider West African market.

The initiative is being developed from Guinea with an international outlook. Each potential collaboration is discussed individually, according to the company's needs and authorization.

A clear framework

A clear mandate. Defined responsibilities.

ODM TECH CONNECT is not a buyer, reseller or distributor of products. The initiative seeks suitable cooperation models with companies, depending on their requirements.

01 · Written authorization

Define the role, responsibilities, territory, objectives and limits in writing.

02 · Build connections

Identify relevant prospects and introduce the company's offer to potential partners.

03 · Refer opportunities

Pass qualified enquiries and interested contacts to the company's authorized team.

04 · Agree on remuneration

Any commission, fee or compensation must be agreed in writing before work begins.

Territorial exclusivity is never assumed. It must be expressly granted in a written agreement.
